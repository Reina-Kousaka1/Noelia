# Training & Preparation

**Status: Training implemented; expanded exercise catalog and Preparation planned.**

Existing Ballet activities include Class, Barre, Center Practice, Stretching,
Technique, Pointe Practice, Rehearsal, Choreography, Performance, Audition,
Recital, and Showcase. Practice grants and cooldowns are transactionally
recorded. Future Preparation steps may affect outcome probability, but
incomplete preparation must never make an attempt impossible. Conditioning and
Gymnastics are intended as core gameplay, not optional guild features.
