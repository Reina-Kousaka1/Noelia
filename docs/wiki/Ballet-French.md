# Ballet French

**Status: Starter lesson implemented; expanded course planned.**

Lessons may teach ballet vocabulary, directions, and common instructions in
increasing complexity. French knowledge can add explanations or enrichment;
French fluency is never a requirement for gameplay success. English remains
the primary language of Madame Noélia, and understandable translations remain
available. `/learn` currently includes introductory lessons on plié, tendu,
and relevé.
