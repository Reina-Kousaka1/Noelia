# Academy History

**Status: Fictional lore and a starter Knowledge lesson implemented.**

Noélia’s lore starts with the fictional Académie de Ballet Noélia and follows
its growth into Maison Noélia — Académie de Ballet. The original Académie
remains the heart of the Maison. Seven content-only chapters are in
`src/ballet/academy-history.ts`; they do not grant Ballet progress or affect
game rules. `/learn` also provides an introductory Academy-history lesson.
